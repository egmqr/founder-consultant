title: Log agent failures and turn repeats into rules
areas: [HAR]
checks: [HAR-1, HAR-11]
---
**Summary.** Agent mistakes that nobody records get paid for again and again. Log every corrected or rejected agent output: the task, what the agent did, what it should have done, who caught it, how much damage, and the fix. Review the log weekly; a mistake caught twice becomes a written rule and a test the agent must pass before acting. The log maps exactly how the business works and breaks, so store it on systems you control and exclude it from vendor training and sharing.

**Takeaways**
- A mistake caught twice is a missing rule.
- Failure logs are valuable, private data.
