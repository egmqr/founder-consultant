**Summary.** AI can produce most of an app quickly, but the remaining part, the part that gets you hacked, isn't fixed by better prompts. It takes a harness: standards the agent can't skip.
1. **Rules:** one short rules file at the repo root, loaded every session, listing the stack, structure, naming and forbidden actions. Every rule should be checkable.
2. **A map:** agents get worse when you dump everything into their context. Give them one page on where things live and what's off limits, kept current.
3. **Proof:** one check script (tests, types, lint, dependency scan) that must pass before anything is called done, with its output in the summary.

**Takeaways**
- A prompt is a request; a harness enforces your standards.
- An agent saying "done" means it feels confident, not that checks passed.
