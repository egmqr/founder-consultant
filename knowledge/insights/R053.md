**Summary.** Staff often sign up for AI assistants with personal accounts and connect them to the company inbox, drive or CRM, with nobody approving or knowing. Consumer plans commonly train on data by default, while business plans usually do not. Build an inventory of every AI tool and account owner, personal and business. Move personal accounts touching company data to a business plan with training off. List every connected app and agent, what it reaches and who approved it, and revoke anything without an owner.

**Takeaways**
- You cannot protect accounts you do not know exist.
- Every connected app is a door; revoke ownerless ones.
