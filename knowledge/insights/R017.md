areas: [DATA]

**Summary.** Pricing logic, client-handling tactics and fixes that worked often live only inside long chat histories that scroll away; the vendor keeps them, the business does not. The first step toward any model change is data capture: whenever something is figured out, write it into a plain file in a folder you own, including why the decision was made. Treat how you make the product as proprietary. With that folder, any new or self-hosted model knows the business from its first prompt.

**Takeaways**
- Chat history is not your knowledge base.
- Write down the reason, not just the decision.
