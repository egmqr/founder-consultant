title: Harden passwordless magic links
areas: [AUTHZ]
checks: [AUTHZ-9]
---
**Summary.** Magic-link sign-in removes passwords but adds its own holes. A redirect parameter that isn't checked lets a crafted link send the freshly authenticated session to an attacker's domain. Links that never expire turn an old mailbox into a pile of valid logins. An unthrottled request endpoint lets anyone flood a victim's inbox from your domain and burn your sender reputation. Allowlist redirect targets, make links expire within minutes and work once, and rate-limit requests per address and per IP.

**Takeaways**
- Post-login redirects go only to allowlisted own domains
- Sign-in links are short-lived and single-use
- Rate-limit link requests per email and per IP
- Password reset links follow the same rules and die when the password or 2FA changes
