title: Conditional access must check more than location
areas: [SEC, EVI]
checks: [SEC-11]
---
**Summary.** Sign-in policies that trust any managed device on the office network let an attacker with phished credentials walk straight in from inside. A managed device is not a compliant one until patch level, antivirus and disk encryption are verified. Policies should also weigh sign-in risk (unfamiliar device, new location, impossible travel) and demand stronger proof or block at medium risk and above. One policy for everything means passing it opens email and the admin console alike, so tier policies by how sensitive the application is.

**Takeaways**
- Network location is not a trust signal
- Require verified device compliance, not just enrolment
- Step up or block on risky sign-ins
- Stricter policies for admin portals and sensitive data
