**Summary.** Many AI-built apps look finished and demo well, but most share three invisible problems: the data lives in the browser (clear it and everything's gone), permissions are checked only on screen, and secrets sit in browser code. Before any customer uses the app, ask whoever built it: Where does the data live? Does the server check who may do what? Where are the keys? Keep building prototypes with AI; just don't call the prototype the product.
