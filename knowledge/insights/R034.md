title: Scope agent jobs, approve actions, end access
areas: [HAR, SEC]
checks: [HAR-2, SEC-4]
---
**Summary.** So-called rogue agents usually did exactly what a person asked, with access that person handed over and no limits. Before any agent touches money or business systems, write down one specific job rather than a vague goal, have it propose actions and wait for approval before acting, and revoke its access as soon as the job is done. The real question after any incident is who gave the agent the keys and what they told it to do.

**Takeaways**
- A vague goal plus full access is the failure.
- Propose, approve, then act.
- Access ends when the job ends.
