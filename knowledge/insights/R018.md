areas: [HAR, VEN]

**Summary.** An installer paid by the vendors he installs works for those vendors, so a free rollout of many AI tools is a sales funnel, not an assessment. Each tool adds a login, a company holding your data, unread terms and an ongoing bill, usually unchecked against existing systems or IP protection. Agents that read email are exposed to prompt injection, the top risk on industry agent-security lists. Insurers increasingly exclude AI use; industry reports cite most exclusion requests approved; verify.

**Takeaways**
- Ask who pays the advisor before trusting the advice.
- Every new AI tool is a new data holder and a new attack path.
