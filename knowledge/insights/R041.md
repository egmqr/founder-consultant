**Summary.** When the firm advising on AI adoption is owned by or paid through a model vendor, its advice will tend to favor that vendor's models, hosting and lock-in. Accept good help, but keep three decisions in house: which model runs which job, where your data lives, and how you would leave. An advisor who cannot explain how you would switch away is selling, not advising.

**Takeaways**
- Ask whose interest the advice serves.
- Own the model-per-job map, the data location and the exit plan.
