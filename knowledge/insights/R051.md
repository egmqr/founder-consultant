title: Get sign-off before connecting AI to email
areas: [VEN]

**Summary.** A common first step for AI consultants is connecting a chat model to the company inbox, which holds deals, contracts, hiring, pricing and disputes: the whole business. Done casually, on default settings, that data may be used to train the provider's models. Anyone advising you should explain, before turning anything on, what will leave the business, where it goes and whether training is disabled, and get written approval. One who skips that conversation has probably never carried liability or compliance.

**Takeaways**
- The inbox is the business, not just mail.
- No connection without a data-flow disclosure and sign-off.
