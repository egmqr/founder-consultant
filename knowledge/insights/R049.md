**Summary.** Instead of outside investors, find an established business with cash flow and the exact problem, and offer a pilot discount or a small equity stake in exchange for funding the build. That customer proves the use case and becomes a reference. Before signing, write down who owns the code, whether you may sell to their competitors, and what happens if they exit; otherwise the first investor becomes the only customer. Founder-replacement rates after funding are high (industry reports cite 88%; verify).

**Takeaways**
- A paying pilot is the best proof.
- Settle code ownership, resale rights and exit before signing.
