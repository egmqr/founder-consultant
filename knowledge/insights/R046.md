title: Tune for behavior; keep knowledge in documents
areas: [MOD]

**Summary.** Fine-tuning shapes how a model behaves, not what it reliably knows, so tuning on company documents means paying to teach facts it will still get wrong and that go stale. Keep pricing, policies and specs in a document store the model reads at answer time, kept current. Tune only for behavior such as tone and output format, using a few hundred clean examples approved by the best person at that task. Prefer an open model you can host, and compare it with the untuned version before release.

**Takeaways**
- Facts go in retrieval, behavior goes in tuning.
- A few hundred clean, expert-approved examples beat thousands of messy ones.
- Test the tuned model against the base before use.
