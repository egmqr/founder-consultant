title: Give staff a sanctioned AI front door
areas: [GOV, HAR]
checks: [GOV-2, HAR-4]
---
**Summary.** Staff paste client data into personal AI accounts when the approved tool is harder to use. Offer a company chat that is easier than a phone app, behind company single sign-on, with accounts created from the staff directory and removed on departure. Route sensitive client or financial data only to models whose terms exclude training, or to a self-hosted model. Keep model choice in one config file with a tested fallback per task. Then block personal AI sites through DNS filtering or device management and review weekly what gets blocked.

**Takeaways**
- Make the approved AI tool easier than the personal one.
- Model choice is one config setting with a tested fallback.
- Repeated block attempts mean the front door is missing.
