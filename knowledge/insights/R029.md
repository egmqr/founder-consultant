**Summary.** Control over AI does not require buying GPUs. Renting a GPU server and running an open-weight model on it keeps the weights, fine-tuning data, settings and keys yours while the host only supplies hardware and power. That differs from calling a hosted API, where the provider decides the model, its price, its support and what happens to your requests. If the host raises prices or fails, copy the model to another provider and keep running.

**Takeaways**
- Own what is inside the building, not the building.
- Portability between hosts is the real test of ownership.
