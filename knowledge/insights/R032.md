**Summary.** AI assistants that remember your preferences, projects and processes become more useful every week, and harder to leave every week; that is a deliberate switching cost. A slightly weaker model that knows your business beats a brilliant one starting from zero. Keep decisions, playbooks and client history in files you own on systems you control, let any model read them, and let no vendor hold the only copy. Then switching models takes an afternoon instead of a month.

**Takeaways**
- Vendor-held memory is rented knowledge.
- Any model may read it; none may keep it.
