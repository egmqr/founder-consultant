**Summary.** Mastering the same few industry AI platforms that every competitor uses is not a moat. First answer what the business knows that others do not: how it prices, qualifies customers and makes the calls rivals get wrong. Buy commodities such as data feeds, distribution and ad networks. Build and own the layer that holds playbooks, configurations and decision logic. Encoding that expertise inside someone else's product risks it training their model and becoming a setting anyone can click.

**Takeaways**
- Tool mastery is not a moat.
- Own the layer that holds what only you know.
