**Summary.** Builders keep tearing down their AI setup every time a new model or tool comes out. The model is only the engine. What lasts is the harness around it: the rules, permissions, tools, project knowledge and the checks that prove work is right before it ships. Rent models; own the harness.

**Takeaways**
- Standards written in your own files, enforced on every agent.
- A new model should be a swap, not a rebuild.
- Owning the layer around the model turns upgrades into gains.
