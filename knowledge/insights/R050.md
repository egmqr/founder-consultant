**Summary.** Many firms cannot stop a misbehaving agent quickly or undo what it did (industry reports cite seven in ten; verify), and acting through an agent does not reduce your liability. Stopping should be one command that revokes agent keys, pauses schedules and ends running jobs. Add breakers before that: provider spend caps, gateway rate limits, and a human approval queue for payments and customer messages. Log each action with how to reverse it, keep a rollback runbook, and pull the switch on a timed monthly drill.

**Takeaways**
- Stopping is one command, not twelve dashboards.
- Spend caps and approval queues catch runaway loops first.
- Stopping is not undoing: log reversal steps and drill.
