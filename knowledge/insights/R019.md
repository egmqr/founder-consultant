**Summary.** Generic advice to start with a receptionist bot or lead generation serves the seller, not the business. The first module shapes what the AI learns and whether people trust it. Choose it by asking three questions: what does everything else depend on, what has a hard deadline, and what could prove value this month to the people who must use it. Options include inventorying systems and data first, or protecting the owner's undocumented playbook before any AI touches it. Then expand one module at a time.

**Takeaways**
- The first module decides whether the rollout is trusted.
- Expand one module at a time, staying in control.
