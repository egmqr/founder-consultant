title: Scrub operational records before training on them
areas: [DATA]
checks: [DATA-7]

**Summary.** The data needed to tailor a model already sits in the business's own systems: support conversations, sales calls, proposals, contracts, invoices and email threads. Export it, clean it and format it for the training tool. Because these records are full of customer names, contact details and confidential terms, the dataset build should be a repeatable script that strips or masks personal and confidential fields and records what went in, and it needs a working export path from every system holding the data.

**Takeaways**
- Support, sales and contract history is usable training data
- Strip personal and confidential fields before training
- Make dataset builds repeatable and logged
