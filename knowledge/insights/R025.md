**Summary.** Calling an open-weight model through someone else's hosted interface still leaves you dependent, yet owning a data center is not required for control. Picture a spectrum: rented and vendor-managed, rented hosting you manage, then owned hardware. Most businesses fit a hybrid: frontier models for heavy tasks reached through a layer you manage, and your own model for anything sensitive to intellectual property. Dependency is not using frontier models; it is having no alternative. Know where you sit and keep moving toward holding more keys.

**Takeaways**
- Dependency means having no alternative.
- Route IP-sensitive work to a model you manage.
