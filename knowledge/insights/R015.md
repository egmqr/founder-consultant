**Summary.** Owning servers for AI is rarely needed; the goal is control, not hardware. Four honest reasons justify buying: data that legally or contractually cannot leave the premises, round-the-clock heavy use where rental costs exceed ownership, latency needs such as real-time voice or factory control that a remote data center cannot meet, and sites with unreliable connectivity. If none apply, rent hosting you manage yourself and keep capital working elsewhere. Track rental spend so the buy point is noticed.

**Takeaways**
- Control is the goal; hardware is one way to hold it.
- Track rental cost against ownership cost.
