**Summary.** Guarding an idea is wasted effort: many others are building the same thing. The new competitor is the customer, who now has the same AI tools and lives inside the problem every day. Your product has to answer why they would pay you instead of building it themselves. The usual answers are time, engineering depth, and someone accountable when it breaks. If nobody is paying yet, there's no business to protect.

**Takeaways**
- Find a person who will pay before guarding or polishing.
- Accountability (support and reliability you stand behind) is part of what customers buy.
- Building proves you know the business; hardening makes it sellable.
