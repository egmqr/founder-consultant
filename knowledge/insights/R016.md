**Summary.** Building is now cheap, so ideas are easy to copy and the old cost moat is gone. Pitching investors early can hand your plan to competitors they already fund. Climb a funding ladder instead: first a paying customer, which proves the idea and costs nothing, then an angel with industry experience, then a business loan, and venture capital last. Industry reports cite most venture-backed founders exiting with nothing and many losing the CEO role within three years; verify.

**Takeaways**
- A paying customer is the first and cheapest proof.
- Share traction in pitches, not the playbook.
