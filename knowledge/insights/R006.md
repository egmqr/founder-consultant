**Summary.** Underwriters don't need you to be perfect, but they do need one file that proves how your AI systems work.
1. A register of every AI tool, agent and automation: data touched, builder, current owner, last outside review.
2. Proof of access control: 2FA everywhere, no shared passwords, agents on their own credentials.
3. Proof of recovery: logging and retention, a backup actually restored, and the date and result of the last drill.
