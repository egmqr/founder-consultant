**Summary.** When you sell software, every customer shares your system, so keeping their data apart isn't a feature; it is the product.
1. **Choose the wall on purpose:** separate stacks per customer (simpler to reason about) or shared tables with a customer ID on every row (cheaper to run).
2. **Enforce it below the app:** row-level security or separate credentials, so a missed filter in code can't leak everything.
3. **Attack yourself on every release:** two test customers, and automated attempts to reach one from the other through every screen, API and export.

**Takeaways**
- Separation you haven't tested is a hope.
- Row-level security is often off by default; turn it on with a policy for every operation and test with a second account
