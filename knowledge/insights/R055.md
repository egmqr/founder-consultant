**Summary.** Run the numbers before committing to a metered frontier API: monthly token volume times current price, projected over one to two years, versus self-hosted compute plus setup and upkeep labor. Example figures put tens of millions of tokens monthly at a few thousand dollars per month against a few hundred for a capable self-hosted model (industry reports cite; verify). Also weigh that prompts carry pricing logic, customer patterns and strategy to the provider, so route sensitive logic to models you control or whose terms you accept.

**Takeaways**
- Project metered API cost against self-hosted cost including labor
- Treat prompts as carrying business logic to the provider
- Revisit the comparison when provider pricing or bundling changes
