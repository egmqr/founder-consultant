**Summary.** Insurers are increasingly excluding AI from business coverage (industry reports cite regulators approving most carrier requests for such exclusions; verify before quoting). That isn't hostility: underwriters can't price what they can't see, and an undocumented AI system built over a weekend is the biggest unknown in a business. Companies that can show who built a system, how it was reviewed, how access is controlled and how it recovers get covered. Ask your insurer two questions, what the policy says about AI and what they'd need to see to cover it, then build exactly that.

**Takeaways**
- Coverage follows provability, not company size.
- Waiting for the rules to change is not a strategy.
- Check the policy for AI exclusions now.
