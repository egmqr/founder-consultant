title: Fix the process before you automate it
areas: [ROLL]

**Summary.** AI does not create process problems; it accelerates them. Many workflows only work because a person remembers which vendor to double-check, reads a group chat, or quietly fixes mismatched numbers each week. An agent knows none of that and will repeat the error at scale all night. Before automating, ask the people doing the work where they check things by hand, what they know that is not written down, and what breaks when they are away. Fix those steps, document them, reconcile the data, then automate.

**Takeaways**
- Automation scales existing mistakes.
- Tacit knowledge is a hidden broken step.
- Documented processes are what AI can learn from.
