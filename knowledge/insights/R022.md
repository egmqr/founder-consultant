title: Count and revoke third-party app grants
areas: [EVI, GOV]
checks: [EVI-2, GOV-4]

**Summary.** Every app connected to a company office suite holds a standing grant to data that skips passwords and second factors, so a breach at that app's maker becomes your breach. Export every connected app with who approved it, what it reaches and when it was last used. Revoke grants unused for 30 days, never admin-approved, or holding full mail, file or admin scope for a smaller job. Then disable user self-consent, require admin approval, and run each new tool through a short checklist.

**Takeaways**
- Every OAuth grant is a key to your systems.
- Without admin approval the list grows back within a week.
