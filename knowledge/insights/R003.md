title: Real conversations beat engagement tricks
areas: [COM]
checks: [COM-1, COM-2, COM-3]
---
**Summary.** Engagement tricks such as keyword prompts, forced follows and gated value inflate numbers that matter less than real use, and they bury the users who actually want to talk. Put the value in the product itself, keep real conversations human (or clearly labelled when automated, with a way to reach a person), and deal with bad-faith users by blocking or removing them, not arguing.

**Takeaways**
- Manufactured engagement looks good on a dashboard; real conversations build trust and keep customers.
- Automated replies that pretend to be human end the conversation, and the trust with it.
