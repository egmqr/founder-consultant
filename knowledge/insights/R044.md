**Summary.** AI labs now pay industry experts to teach models the judgment that was never written down, and staff may already be doing this on the side. That tacit judgment, the quick call a senior person makes, is often the most valuable asset in a business and is rarely captured. Record how your best people decide, as rules and approved examples, in a system you own, so the business benefits from it first and it survives staff turnover.

**Takeaways**
- Tacit judgment is an asset with a market price.
- Write decisions down as rules and examples you own.
