**Summary.** Before rebuilding anything with AI, take a snapshot of what you already run. Start from money, not memory: read a year of card, bank and accounting records for every recurring software charge with amount, cycle and renewal date. Then export user lists and last sign-in dates and flag seats unused for 60 days. Finally map each system to its owner, the workflow it serves, the data it holds and whether that data can leave by API or export, sorted by renewal date.

**Takeaways**
- Owners undercount software; billing records do not.
- Unused seats are donations.
- Know whether your data can get out.
