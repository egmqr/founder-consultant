**Summary.** Buying a business AI plan that excludes training protects only that plan. Much workplace AI use runs on staff personal accounts, where defaults often allow training on pasted emails, customer data and financials; industry reports cite roughly two thirds of usage on personal accounts; verify. Regulators already fine firms over business done on personal devices and are examining AI monitoring. A ban fails against habit, because staff just want to work faster, so supply a better sanctioned tool and measure actual usage.

**Takeaways**
- Your paid plan does not protect personal accounts.
- A ban fails where a better tool succeeds.
