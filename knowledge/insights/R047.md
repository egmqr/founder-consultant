title: Assume shipped code can be read
areas: [SEC]
checks: [SEC-1]
---
**Summary.** With AI tools, firmware, mobile apps and published specs can be taken apart in hours by anyone. Treat everything that runs on a customer's device as readable. What cannot be copied is what stays inside: decision-making, data, customer relationships, improvement speed and how you respond when things break. Many businesses leak exactly that by pasting it into chat tools, handing it to consultants on day one, or letting it leave with departing staff.

**Takeaways**
- Shipped code is not a secret.
- Protect data, decisions and relationships that never leave the building.
