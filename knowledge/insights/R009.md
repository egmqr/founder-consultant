title: Give in-house builders a door, not a back window
areas: [CHG]
checks: [CHG-1, CHG-2, CHG-3]
---
**Summary.** Staff are already building tools with AI on their own laptops, often with company data out of sight. Don't try to stop them; the people doing the work build the best tools. Give them a sanctioned path instead:
1. One company repository under company accounts with 2FA, so tools stay when people leave.
2. Realistic sample data for building, with real data kept behind review.
3. A protected main branch: pull requests, automatic secret scanning, one named reviewer, and a record of who approved each release.

**Takeaways**
- A better tool beats a better policy.
- Ban it and it moves to personal phones.
