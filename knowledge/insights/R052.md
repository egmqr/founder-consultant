**Summary.** Domain experts hold knowledge that is in no public training set, and that is what makes it valuable. Pouring it into someone else's chat platform leaves every insight on their servers under their terms. Building the engine yourself, with your documents, rules and model behind your own interface, turns the knowledge into a scalable business asset. AI is valuable to an expert because it carries what they know further, but only if they own the vessel.

**Takeaways**
- Your knowledge is the asset; AI scales it.
- Keep documents, rules and the interface under your control.
