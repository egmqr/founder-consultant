title: Measure AI by workflow outcomes, not seats

**Summary.** Rolling out AI as a license per employee and reporting who logged in can make people faster while cost rises and profit stays flat. Treat the workflow as the unit: pick the one costing the most time or money, rebuild it where the data already lives, keep the data inside the business, and measure hours, errors and money before and after. Expand only once the first workflow proves out. Large vendors report deals growing this way (industry reports cite; verify).

**Takeaways**
- Logins are not a return on investment.
- Baseline one costly workflow, rebuild it, measure, then expand.
