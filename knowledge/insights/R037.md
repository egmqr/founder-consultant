**Summary.** AI agents did not create today's vulnerabilities; they find existing ones in minutes rather than weeks: unpatched servers, forgotten files, unlocked doors. Audits of business applications, human-built and AI-built alike, commonly come back with serious security findings (industry reports cite; verify). Patching an old system bolted onto decades of integrations is often harder than rebuilding it properly, but an AI-generated copy of an old system inherits the same holes. Design security in from the first line and audit every feature going in and out.

**Takeaways**
- Attack speed changed, not the bugs.
- A vibe-coded copy is a new old system.
- Audit each feature on the way in.
