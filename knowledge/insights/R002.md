**Summary.** A growing share of breaches comes through third parties (industry breach reports cite a sharp rise in 2026; verify before quoting). Many new AI agencies are under a year old, so no customer has seen them support an agent through year two. Ask "what did you do before this agency?" The good answer is a history of deploying, training and supporting software in real companies. Then talk to a customer of more than a year.

**Takeaways**
- AI vendors touch invoices, email, customers and contracts: vet them like any vendor with data access.
- Plan who supports the system after launch.
- Familiarity is not qualification.
