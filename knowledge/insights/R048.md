title: Know what you sell before exporting data
areas: [DATA]

**Summary.** Buyers are offering businesses money for old emails, support tickets and records because that history shows how work actually gets done, which is what agents need to learn your job. Such offers shrink once a sector is covered, and the data never comes back once it trains a model that competitors then rent. Selling the whole business can be reasonable; selling only the data is a different, one-way deal. Review exactly what is included and whether customer agreements allow it before exporting anything.

**Takeaways**
- Data sold for training never comes back.
- Selling the data is not the same as selling the business.
