**Summary.** A product that touches personal data falls under privacy laws wherever its users live, from the first visitor, and AI builds rarely add any of it. Three documents should exist before revenue: a privacy policy describing what is collected and why, terms of service, and a data processing agreement for every third party that handles user data. Draft them from the actual data flows, not a template, and get a short legal review. Low-cost compliance tooling now automates evidence collection and security questionnaires (industry reports cite entry prices near a few hundred dollars a month; verify). Rules on cookies, consent and data residency keep changing, so a short quarterly review per jurisdiction keeps the documents true.

**Takeaways**
- Privacy policy, terms and processor agreements exist before launch
- Documents match the real data flows and third parties
- A dated quarterly review covers each user jurisdiction
