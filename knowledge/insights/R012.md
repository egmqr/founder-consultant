**Summary.** Replacing one rented platform with a smaller vendor's rented wrapper only swaps one landlord for a riskier one. Before signing, ask whether the system keeps running, and stays entirely yours, if the vendor is let go tomorrow. Ownership means the code, data, workflows and infrastructure accounts sit in your name, the build is handed over in production, and support is optional rather than a lock-in. A shared multi-client platform also means your workflows may sit beside competitors.

**Takeaways**
- Ask: if we fire you tomorrow, does it still run and is it ours?
- A new subscription wrapper is a new landlord, not control.
